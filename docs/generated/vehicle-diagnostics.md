# Vehicle Diagnostics

Status: software-validated-hardware-pending
Access: Home > Vehicle Diagnostics

## What it does
Reads standard engine/emissions codes, readiness, freeze frames and live values; records and exports evidence with an offline DTC library.

## When to use it
Investigate check-engine conditions, compare scans and capture changing readings without an internet connection.

## Why it is useful
Local display, console and authenticated browser share one cancellable adapter owner and source-aware explanations.

## Limits and effects
Vehicle and adapter support varies. Standard engine/emissions only. USB, Classic SPP and vehicle decoding await physical adapter/vehicle testing. Community definitions remain unverified; package activation remains gated.
