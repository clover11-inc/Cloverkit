# Wi-Fi target tracking

Status: alpha-hardware-validation-pending
Access: Targets > Wi-Fi Targets

## What it does
Tracks selected APs, observed clients and probes.

## When to use it
Focus on a discovered target.

## Why it is useful
Continuous signal and freshness feedback.

## Limits and effects
Signal trend is not distance or guaranteed direction.
