# USB device inventory

Status: alpha-software-validation
Access: System

## What it does
Enumerates connected USB hardware and available adapters.

## When to use it
Identify connected field peripherals.

## Why it is useful
Makes adapter availability visible before starting a workflow.

## Limits and effects
USB product labels do not establish chipset/driver capabilities.
